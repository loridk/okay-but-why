Episode guide

Containers & Infrastructure Series

Start at Episode 1 or jump directly to any Containers & Infrastructure topic. Each page includes the audio, transcript, and code cards.

  • Episode 1: Where Does My App Actually Live?Computers, runtimes, processes, ports, and servers; distinguish source files from running software and connect local development to traditional hosting and production.
  • Episode 2: Servers, VMs & the Cloud Before ContainersPhysical servers, shared hosting, VPSes, hypervisors, and virtual machines; understand isolation, resource sharing, and why virtualization became useful.
  • Episode 3: Docker: Who Asked for This?The environment problems behind “it works on my machine”; containers, shared kernels, Docker tooling, and how containers differ from virtual machines.
  • Episode 4: Images, Containers & DockerfilesDockerfile → image → container; base images, layers, registries, building, pulling, and running, with an annotated Dockerfile and lifecycle diagram.
  • Episode 5: Why Can't My Container See Localhost?!Localhost, container networking, port mappings, configuration, secrets, volumes, and persistence; understand who is making a request and where the data lives.
  • Episode 6: Docker Compose: My App Has FriendsDescribe a frontend, API, and database together using Compose YAML, networks, and volumes; connect the underlying concepts to DDEV and Lando.
  • Episode 7: From Git Push to ProductionFollow code through CI checks, image builds, registries, and deployment; explore production configuration, health checks, logging, and the limits of rollback.
  • Episode 8: Kubernetes: Who Asked for This?!Why coordinating workloads across many machines becomes its own problem; introduce orchestration, scheduling, scaling, recovery, discovery, and desired state.
  • Episode 9: Pods, Nodes & Clusters, Oh MyClusters, nodes, Pods, Deployments, and Services; follow desired replicas versus actual replicas through reconciliation, health probes, and rolling updates.
  • Episode 10: The Cloud Is Someone Else's Computer*Compute, storage, databases, networking, CDNs, managed containers, and serverless; compare cloud service categories, operational responsibilities, costs, and control.
  • Episode 11: Infrastructure as Code: Why Is My Server in Git?Configuration drift, declarative provisioning, version control, and plan/apply workflows; explain Terraform, OpenTofu, sensitive state, and repeatable environments.
  • Episode 12: Do You Actually Need Any of This Shit?Compare infrastructure choices for portfolios, CMS sites, APIs, SaaS, and distributed systems; choose tools by requirements, recovery needs, cost, and operational ownership.
