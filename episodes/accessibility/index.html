Episode guide

Accessibility Series

Start at Episode 1 or jump directly to any Accessibility topic. Each page includes the audio, transcript, and code cards.

  • Episode 1: Who Is This Actually For? Different ways of perceiving and operating the web; overlapping barriers, assistive technology, permanent, temporary, and situational limitations, WCAG, POUR, and conformance versus usability.
  • Episode 2: HTML Was Already Doing This The DOM-to-accessibility-tree mental model; native semantics and behavior, links, buttons, headings, lists, landmarks, labels, groups, and tables.
  • Episode 3: Throw Away Your Mouse Keyboard patterns, focus, focus indicators and order, tabindex, skip links, dialogs, dynamic changes, SPA navigation, and composite-widget responsibilities.
  • Episode 4: ARIA: What Is This Shit Actually For? Native-first decisions; ARIA roles, states, properties, names, descriptions, relationships, live regions, hiding, and the difference between adding semantics and adding behavior.
  • Episode 5: Your Design Is Lying to You Contrast, color-independent meaning, zoom, reflow, text spacing, reduced motion, target size, persistent labels, useful errors, and cognitive load.
  • Episode 6: Your Lighthouse Score Is Not an Accessibility Audit Lighthouse, axe, WAVE, browser inspectors, static tooling, interaction tests, keyboard and focus testing, zoom, screen readers, and the human judgment automated tools cannot replace.
