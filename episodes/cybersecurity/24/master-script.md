## Red Team

## Purple Team

## Careers Are Not a Ladder

PARISA: Do you have to start in help desk, then SOC, then pentest, then security engineer?

JULES: No.

PARISA: Developers can move into AppSec, product security, security tooling, cloud security.

JULES: Systems admins may move into infrastructure security.

Auditors into GRC.

Analysts into detection or incident response.

PARISA: Existing technical background matters.

JULES: Very much.
