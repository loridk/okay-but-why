Episode guide

Cybersecurity Series

Cybersecurity for developers, with broad Security+ coverage hiding behind the scenes so the show can focus on understanding instead of acronym soup.

  • Episode 1: Cybersecurity — What Are We Actually Protecting? Confidentiality, integrity, availability, assets, threats, vulnerabilities, risk, attack surfaces, and why security is really about protecting systems people depend on.
  • Episode 2: Networking, But Now Everyone Is Suspicious IP addresses, ports, protocols, DNS, TCP and UDP, packets, common network services, and how ordinary networking becomes part of an attack surface.
  • Episode 3: Firewalls, Segmentation, VPNs & Zero Trust Firewalls, network segmentation, VLANs, DMZs, VPNs, proxies, defense in depth, least privilege, and what zero trust actually means.
  • Episode 4: Authentication — Prove You're You Passwords, password managers, MFA, biometrics, authentication factors, password attacks, account recovery, and why identity proof is harder than it looks.
  • Episode 5: Authorization & IAM — Are You Allowed to Do That? Authentication versus authorization, identity and access management, roles, permissions, least privilege, access-control models, provisioning, and privilege escalation.
  • Episode 6: Cryptography Without the Wizard Robes Encryption, hashing, symmetric and asymmetric cryptography, keys, salts, signatures, integrity, confidentiality, and why encoding is not encryption.
  • Episode 7: HTTPS, TLS, Certificates & PKI How HTTPS uses TLS, certificates, certificate authorities, public and private keys, trust chains, handshakes, and PKI to protect connections.
  • Episode 8: Social Engineering — Hacking the Squishy Humans Phishing, spear phishing, impersonation, pretexting, baiting, tailgating, urgency, authority, trust, and why humans are part of every security system.
  • Episode 9: Malware, Ransomware & Other Assholes Viruses, worms, trojans, ransomware, spyware, rootkits, botnets, persistence, command and control, infection paths, and defensive layers.
  • Episode 10: How Attacks Actually Happen Reconnaissance, initial access, exploitation, persistence, privilege escalation, lateral movement, exfiltration, impact, and attack-chain thinking.
  • Episode 11: XSS — You Put WHAT in My DOM? Reflected, stored, and DOM-based cross-site scripting; output encoding, unsafe DOM APIs, sanitization, Content Security Policy, and treating untrusted content as data.
  • Episode 12: Injection — Stop Concatenating Untrusted Shit SQL injection, command injection, parameterized queries, prepared statements, input handling, validation, and why string concatenation creates dangerous boundaries.
  • Episode 13: CSRF, CORS & Same-Origin Policy — Why Is the Browser Yelling at Me? Same-origin policy, CORS, CSRF, preflight requests, credentials, CSRF tokens, SameSite cookies, and the different problems these browser protections solve.
  • Episode 14: Sessions, Cookies, Tokens & API Security Sessions, cookies, bearer tokens, JWTs, expiration, rotation, secure storage, API authorization, object-level access control, rate limiting, and secret handling.
  • Episode 15: OWASP & Threat Modeling — Please Imagine the Worst OWASP, common application risks, threat modeling, trust boundaries, data flows, abuse cases, STRIDE, and thinking about attacks before somebody else does.
  • Episode 16: Dependencies, Supply Chains & Secrets Third-party dependencies, package ecosystems, lockfiles, dependency scanning, compromised packages, software supply chains, secrets, credentials, and repository hygiene.
  • Episode 17: Vulnerabilities, CVEs & Pentesting Vulnerabilities, CVEs, CVSS, scanning, penetration testing, responsible disclosure, remediation, false positives, and prioritizing findings by actual risk.
  • Episode 18: Secure Architecture & Cloud Security Defense in depth, secure defaults, isolation, shared responsibility, cloud IAM, storage permissions, security groups, encryption, configuration, and minimizing blast radius.
  • Episode 19: Security Operations — Who Watches the Logs? Logging, monitoring, alerting, SIEM, detection, indicators of compromise, baselines, telemetry, false positives, and turning system activity into useful security signals.
  • Episode 20: Incident Response — Production Is on Fire and Security Joined Slack Preparation, detection, analysis, containment, eradication, recovery, communication, lessons learned, evidence preservation, and responding without making the incident worse.
  • Episode 21: Digital Forensics — Don't Touch Anything Evidence collection, preservation, chain of custody, disk and memory artifacts, timestamps, logs, forensic integrity, and why casually poking the compromised machine is a bad plan.
  • Episode 22: Risk, Governance, Privacy & Compliance Risk assessment, likelihood and impact, controls, policies, governance, privacy, data classification, regulatory requirements, compliance, and why compliant does not automatically mean secure.
  • Episode 23: Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery Business impact analysis, backups, redundancy, recovery objectives, disaster recovery, continuity planning, testing, and keeping essential services alive when things go sideways.
  • Episode 24: Secure Development & DevSecOps — Security Before Friday Secure development practices, threat modeling, code review, dependency and secret scanning, CI/CD security, SAST, DAST, environment separation, and moving security into the development lifecycle.
  • Episode 25: So You Want to Take Security+ Connect the concepts from the series to Security+ domains, terminology, scenario questions, study strategy, and understanding the material instead of memorizing acronym soup.
