Companion reference

A CMS manages information and the people and processes that keep it useful. The public page is one output of that work.

Translate the terminology

Episode 1 reference
TermUseful mental model
ContentThe information people maintain, such as an event date or description.
PresentationHow information is arranged and rendered for a particular audience.
RoleA grouping of permissions; the exact policy still needs design.
RevisionA saved version of a record, not a full-system backup.
WorkflowAllowed states and transitions in the editorial process.
CMS / framework / site builder / hostContent operations / application construction / page composition / execution environment; products may overlap.

Bellweather example

Moss After Dark is a fictional university event. Its date and location are structured values; its description remains prose. One record supports a detail page, a list, and other consumers.
