Episode guide

Modern CMS Development Series

Start at Episode 1 or jump directly to any Modern CMS Development topic. Each page includes the audio, transcript, and code cards.

  • Episode 1: What the Hell Is a CMS? Why content management exists; structured information, users, revisions, media, workflows, and publishing. Distinguish CMS, framework, site builder, and host.
  • Episode 2: WordPress: How Did a Blogging Tool Eat the Web? Publishing roots, posts/pages/custom post types, taxonomies, database storage, themes/templates, plugins, actions/filters, and blocks; assess ecosystem benefits and architectural fit.
  • Episode 3: Drupal: Why Is Everything an Entity? Entity type → bundle → entity → fields, using nodes, media, taxonomy, users, and blocks. Separate content/configuration entities, references, revisions, and custom-entity decisions.
  • Episode 4: Content Modeling: Why Drupal People Won't Shut Up About Structure Model Bellweather University's people, departments, programs, courses, events, news, and locations. Explain cardinality, references, reuse, taxonomy, view/form modes, moderation, and multilingual decisions.
  • Episode 5: Views, Display Modes & Render Arrays: Who Actually Builds the Page? Views queries, relationships, filters, sorting, displays, and contextual/exposed inputs; trace rendering and explain cache tags, contexts, max-age, and metadata propagation.
  • Episode 6: Modern Drupal Frontend: Twig, Themes & Components Themes, Twig, suggestions, preprocessing, attributes, regions, libraries, and behaviors. Build the mental model for SDC props, slots, colocated assets, accessible markup, and Drupal rendering integration.
  • Episode 7: Modules, Hooks, Services & Symfony: Wait, Which Part Is Drupal? Routes/controllers, plugins, services, dependency injection, Symfony, and events. Compare procedural hooks with Drupal 11.1+ attribute-based OOP hooks while retaining documented procedural exceptions.
  • Episode 8: Composer, Config & Deployment: “But I Changed It in the Admin UI” Composer manifests/locks, Drush, YAML export/import, code versus configuration versus content, environment overrides, staging, updates, deployment, CI/CD, and recovery.
  • Episode 9: APIs, Headless Drupal & Modern Drupal JSON:API resources, serialization, authentication/access, separate frontend caches, previews, and decoupling tradeoffs. Introduce Recipes and distinguish core, Drupal CMS, and Canvas; bridge to the API series.
  • Episode 10: Drupal 10/11: What Changed, What Stayed & Should We Use a CMS? Synthesize enduring Drupal concepts and modernization; compare static sites, WordPress, Drupal, custom applications, and headless CMS approaches through requirements. Close with “What problem are we actually solving?”
