Version snapshot: September 16, 2026

  • Drupal 11.4 is the stable feature line discussed here.
  • Drupal 10.6 is the final Drupal 10 minor line; the published end-of-life date is December 9, 2026.
  • OOP hooks were introduced in 11.1, with procedural exceptions.
  • SDC became stable in 10.3.
  • Drupal CMS and Canvas have release tracks distinct from core.
