Streaming lifecycle

  1. Show “Generating” without moving focus.
  2. Accumulate provider events according to their contract.
  3. Render partial text safely; do not execute partial proposals.
  4. Announce completion or failure at a useful granularity.
  5. Preserve the question; support cancellation and retry deliberately.

First output latency differs from complete-answer latency. Do not announce every token to a screen reader. This example is intentionally non-streaming to isolate the request boundary.
