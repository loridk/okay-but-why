Tokenization: deliberately schematic

Refunds? could be pictured as [Refund][s][?] or another split. This is an illustration, not the measured output of a named tokenizer. Whitespace, punctuation, code, and language affect counts.

Hallucination / confabulation
A plausible but unsupported or false generated claim; a failure description, not a guarantee about its cause.

Two sources of apparent knowledge

  • Learned during training: encoded in parameters; not a dependable database of cited records.
  • Supplied now: policy text, messages, and other available context; not a weight update.

Models can sometimes memorize text, but generation is not a reliable lookup interface. Use authoritative systems for current facts.
