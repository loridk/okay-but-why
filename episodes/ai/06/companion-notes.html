Embedding intuition

Text → a learned list of numbers → similarity comparison. A two-dimensional sketch might place “parcel late” near “missed delivery,” but real embedding spaces have many dimensions without neat human labels. Vectors from incompatible models should not be compared as if they share coordinates.

Choose by the information problem
ApproachGood fitLimit
Keyword searchExact codes, IDs, terminologyCan miss paraphrases.
Semantic searchRelated wording and meaningSimilarity does not prove applicability.
Hybrid retrievalCombine lexical and semantic signalsMore tuning and evaluation.
Direct prompt contextA small known relevant documentManual selection or large inputs may not scale.
RAGSelect current evidence for generationRetrieval and generation can both fail.
Fine-tuningAdapt behavior or task performanceChanges weights; not a simple live-policy store.

Chunking example

Keep a refund rule with its exclusions, effective date, and heading. Arbitrarily splitting before “except” can produce a misleading retrieved passage. Overlap can help but adds repetition. Preserve source IDs and useful context; evaluate the resulting answers.

Download the TypeScript example. The retriever and generator are supplied by the caller; this is not a vector database or complete RAG implementation. Its mock tests check no-evidence behavior and evidence forwarding, not real retrieval quality.
