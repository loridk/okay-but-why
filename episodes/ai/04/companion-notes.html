Shape, meaning, authority

  1. Parse and validate the response shape at runtime.
  2. Check that source IDs exist and support the claims.
  3. Apply authorization and business rules independently.

Schema-constrained output features vary by provider and model. A valid shape is not factual correctness. Low temperature is not a truth guarantee. Prompting changes input; training changes parameters.

Untrusted content

Documents and tool results can contain hostile instructions. Labels and delimiters help express boundaries but do not enforce permissions. Render generated text safely; review generated HTML and alt text for security, accuracy, and accessibility.
