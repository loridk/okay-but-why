Episode guide

AI Series

Start at Episode 1 or jump directly to any AI topic. Each page includes the audio, transcript, and code cards.

  • Episode 1: What the Hell Is AI, Actually?AI, machine learning, deep learning, generative AI, and LLMs; introduce Support Assistant and define the problem before choosing the technology.
  • Episode 2: How Does a Machine Learn Anything?Training data, models, weights, neural networks, and generalization; distinguish training from inference and examine what “train our own AI” actually involves.
  • Episode 3: LLMs: Extremely Fancy Autocomplete?Tokens, next-token prediction, transformers, attention, and context; understand how language models generate responses and why fluent answers are not verified facts.
  • Episode 4: Prompts, Context & Talking to the RobotInstructions, examples, context windows, randomness, and structured output; write useful prompts and explain why Brenda’s 40,000 documents do not belong in every request.
  • Episode 5: Putting AI in Software: Models, APIs & SDKsBrowser → backend → model provider; APIs, SDKs, streaming, model choice, latency, and token costs, with secure API keys and accessible response handling.
  • Episode 6: RAG: Giving AI a Filing CabinetRetrieval, embeddings, vectors, semantic search, chunking, and grounding; compare RAG with keyword search and fine-tuning while keeping evidence current and authorized.
  • Episode 7: Tools & Function Calling: Letting the Robot Do ThingsTool definitions, schemas, arguments, and results; follow application-controlled execution from a read-only order lookup to permissions, approval, and risky write actions.
  • Episode 8: MCP: Why Does AI Need Another Damn Protocol?MCP hosts, clients, servers, tools, resources, and prompts; compare APIs, tool calling, and standardized integrations without confusing connectivity with trust.
  • Episode 9: Agents: Apparently the Robot Has a Job NowChatbots, tool assistants, workflows, and agentic systems; explore planning, state, orchestration, stopping conditions, and when an ordinary if statement is better.
  • Episode 10: Evals, Hallucinations & Keeping the Robot From Ruining TuesdayHallucinations, evaluation datasets, human and automated review, monitoring, and regression checks; test prompt injection, data leakage, authorization, and unsafe actions.
  • Episode 11: From Demo to Production: Oh Shit, People Are Actually Using ItLatency, costs, rate limits, caching, fallbacks, privacy, and operational ownership; compare hosted and local/open-weight models, GPUs, and quantization.
  • Episode 12: Forward Deployed: Turning 'We Should Use AI' Into Something UsefulFollow discovery through implementation, evaluation, production, adoption, and feedback; explain Forward Deployed Engineering and compare related roles through the Support Assistant journey.
